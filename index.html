    Help

    Getting a song in

    Create splits a file, or a YouTube link, into stems. Library opens a song you already split. Import loads stem files you already have. Demo plays a short example so you can try the mixer.

    Play, speed, key, and loop

    The round button plays and pauses (Space). The time shows where you are. Speed changes how fast the song plays and leaves the key alone. Key moves the song up or down and leaves the speed alone. Fit my voice suggests a key for the voice you pick. Loop (L) repeats a section. A and B set the start and end of that section, and Clear removes them. Enter or Home jumps back to the start.

    Each stem

    Every stem has M (mute that stem), S (play only that stem), and a volume slider. The picture beside the slider is there so you can see the sound and click it to jump. Pressing M or S turns Karaoke, Acapella, and Rhythm off. The mute or solo you just set stays.

    Karaoke, Acapella, and Rhythm

    These three are switches. Only one can be on. Click the one that is lit to turn it off. They do not move the volume sliders.

    • Karaoke (K) mutes the vocals. Click it again and the vocals come back.
    • Acapella plays only the vocals. Click it again and the other stems come back.
    • Rhythm plays only the drums, including the separate drum parts, and the bass. Click it again and the rest come back.

    Reset puts every stem volume back to the middle, clears mute and solo, and turns those switches off.

    The highway and Sing along

    The picture above the seek bar shows the notes coming toward a line that means now. Click it to jump there. A chord line appears along the top when a published chord chart for this song agrees with the bass. The app looks that chart up from the song's name. Chord sheet is where you can type the chords you do know, by part: verse G Em and chorus C G# F#. You do not count the lines. The app finds the beat and the meter first. A note the bass plays between beats does not move the chord if the rhythm guitar is still holding it. The parts can repeat. Sing along stays under that picture. It uses your microphone, with Level, a guide tone, and Mic timing if your voice looks a little early or late.

    Lyrics

    When the words have timing, you see four lines: the one just sung, the line being sung, and the next two. The current line fills in as it is sung. Click a line to jump to it. If the words are a little early or late, use −0.5s and +0.5s. Words without timing stay as a scrolling list. Find lyrics searches by artist and title.

    Vocal training, master, and export

    The panel on the right records your voice: Analyze, Mic, the level meter, monitor, Record, Stop, the timer, clear, and download, plus Target, You, and Accuracy. Original, Mine, and Both choose what you hear. Master is the overall left and right level and the overall volume. Export writes a wav of the full mix, a karaoke mix, or the vocals, and can use only the loop region.

    Where the songs live

    Library shows the folder the songs are in. Change folder points the app at another one, for example a drive that already has your songs, and remembers it. A song whose files cannot be opened stays in the list.

    Space plays. K turns Karaoke on or off. L turns the loop on or off. Escape closes this page and the other windows.
